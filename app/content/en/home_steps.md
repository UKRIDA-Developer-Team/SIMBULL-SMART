**How to use it**

- **Recommendation → One student**: fill in the short questionnaire (same questions as the Google Form) or enter feature values directly, then press *Get recommendation*.
- **Recommendation → Many students**: upload a Google Form export or a CSV to get a priority list for every student, then download it.
- **Analysis**: explore the data, model performance, SMART results, sensitivity tests and fairness checks.
- The **weight profile** in the sidebar changes how SMART weighs the criteria; results update at once.
