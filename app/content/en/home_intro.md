**Problem.** Student anxiety often goes unnoticed by students, teachers and parents. Screening relies on a small number of school counselors (guru BK); it is reactive, and the choice of intervention is often subjective.

**Solution.** This app combines **Data Mining** with a **Decision Support System (DSS)**:

1. A **Random Forest** predicts an anxiety score (0-10) from 11 lifestyle and academic factors, without asking about anxiety symptoms directly.
2. The **SMART** method turns that prediction and the factors into a **priority score** (who is helped first) and a **ranking of five interventions**.
3. Every result comes with **reasons**, a **weight sensitivity test**, and a **safety rule** for severe cases.

**Users.** School counselors and homeroom teachers, for early screening and program planning; students, for guided self-reflection.
