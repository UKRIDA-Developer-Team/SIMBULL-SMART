# Question merge report: questionnaire -> model features -> SMART criteria

Source: `data/private/gform_data.csv` (the real, confidential export; aggregates only), 304 rows, 68 columns. Form versions: original 195, revised 109.
Generated by `scripts/merge_report.py` from `spk/gform.py` and `spk/gform_mapping.json`.

## Answer

Yes. The questions are merged before the DSS uses them. `gform.derive` runs first and turns the answers into the 11 model features; SMART (`smart.utility`) only sees those features and the Random Forest prediction, never a raw answer.

- 18 questions feed the 11 model features. 4 features are merged from several questions; the other 7 use one question each.
- 7 Psikologis questions are merged into the target `anxiety_score`. They are never model features, so the target does not leak into the prediction.
- Of the 8 SMART criteria, 2 are merged directly from several questions (`family_expectation`, `burnout_score`), 5 use one question each, and `rf_anxiety_score` is the model output built from all 11 features.
- "Saya mampu membicarakan kesulitan ... dengan keluarga" (`family_talk`, revised form) is no longer merged into `family_expectation`: on the 109 revised rows it lowered Cronbach's alpha from 0.53 to 0.37. It is kept as an extra column.
- `exam_pressure` and `stress_level` are merged features that are not SMART criteria. They reach the decision only inside `rf_anxiety_score`.
- Merge rule: each Likert answer goes to 0-1 (reversed where marked), the items of one feature are averaged, and the mean is scaled to the Kaggle range. A row answered on the original form skips the revised-only items, so its mean uses the original items.

## 1. SMART criteria and where they come from

| SMART criterion | Type | Comes from | Questions | Merged? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rf_anxiety_score` | benefit | Random Forest prediction from all 11 model features | 18 | yes (model output) |
| `study_hours_per_day` | benefit | hours as answered | 1 | no, 1 question |
| `academic_performance` | cost | grade as answered (0-100) | 1 | no, 1 question |
| `sleep_hours` | cost | hours as answered | 1 | no, 1 question |
| `physical_activity` | cost | day band -> days per week (0-7) | 1 | no, 1 question |
| `financial_stress` | benefit | 10 x (5 - answer) / 4, reversed (0-10) | 1 | no, 1 question |
| `family_expectation` | benefit | 10 x mean of the items (0-10) | 2 | yes, 2 questions |
| `burnout_score` | benefit | 10 x mean of the items (0-10) | 3 | yes, 3 questions |

Type: benefit = a higher value raises the priority; cost = a lower value raises the priority.
